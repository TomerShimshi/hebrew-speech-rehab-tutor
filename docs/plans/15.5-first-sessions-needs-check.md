# Sub-plan 15.5: The first sessions find out what he needs

Part of the [master design](00-master-design.md). After [15](15-new-accounts.md), before [16](16-memory-built-by-code.md). Requested by Tomer (2026-10-15); extended the same day with the inferred profile (from 15). **Built 2026-10-15.**

## Why
- **The intro plan only collects names.** Today an account's first session (`prompts/intro_plan.yaml`) gets to know him and seeds the memory with his people and places. Everything after it assumes what to practice: the profile says so (a written one), or nothing does.
- **A new account has no profile (15) unless the family uploads one.** The app has to learn not only who he is, but **what he needs to practice**, and how hard to start -- and write it down as his profile.
- **A speech therapist's first meeting does exactly this,** without a full formal test:
  - **a case history:** what happened and when, the therapy he gets, how he spoke before, hearing and vision, the people he talks with, what he wants to be able to do;
  - **a short informal check of each area:** free conversation (a language sample), naming, repetition, understanding (instructions, yes/no questions), connected speech (describing, telling something in order), and speech clarity (motor speech);
  - **his own goals:** what is hardest in daily life. Formal tests give type and severity, but not what matters to him and his family; needs-based tools ask that directly (e.g. the Aphasia Needs Assessment, Garrett & Beukelman 2006).
  - A widely used framing (A-FROM) asks every assessment to cover four areas: the language impairment, his participation in life situations, his communication environment, and his identity and feelings.
- **Short batteries exist for exactly this kind of quick, many-sided check** (e.g. the Quick Aphasia Battery, ~15 minutes, [Wilson et al. 2018](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5806902/)). Hebrew tools exist too (e.g. Biran & Friedmann's naming test). The app doesn't copy any of them: it borrows the *areas* and the *kinds of tasks*, inside a friendly conversation.

## Goal
1. **The first two sessions of a new account include a gentle needs check**, inside "getting to know you": it must never feel like a test.
2. **The result is his profile, inferred:** a few lines about him, his own names, and a needs profile -- per area, a level, with the evidence (his words from the transcript).
3. **The plan builder uses it** to choose the practice areas and the starting difficulty.
4. **The family sees it and can correct it** on the caregiver page, and add what his real therapist says.
5. **It is not a diagnosis**, and says so wherever it's shown.

## Design

### A. The areas
| Area | What she does (one small step each, inside the conversation) | What the app can practice today |
|---|---|---|
| **Conversation** (fluency, word-finding in talk) | Already the whole session: listen to how he talks | Yes (threads, activities) |
| **Naming** | A few easy "how do you call..." questions about everyday things, then a few about his own world | Yes (names game, practice) |
| **Repetition** | "Can you say this after me?": a word, a short phrase, a longer sentence | Not yet |
| **Understanding** | A simple yes/no question with a twist ("is a stone heavier than a feather?"); a two-part request ("tell me your favorite food, and then the town you grew up in") | Not yet (noted only) |
| **Connected speech / order** | "How do you make coffee?" (a procedure) -- also his first sequencing baseline (14) | Yes (sequencing, activities) |
| **Speech clarity** (motor speech) | Noted from how he sounds throughout; one playful "פה-טה-קה, as fast as you can" | Not yet (noted only) |
| **His goals** | "What's hardest for you when you talk with people? What would you like to be able to do?" | Feeds every plan |

- **Spread over the first two sessions**, about 3-4 small checks each, between ordinary conversation. Never two checks in a row. If he tires, the rest waits for the next session.
- **Each check is a step class** (`app/steps.py`, like 13.5's): its content, its RULES (how to ask, how to react: always warm, never "wrong", help after a few seconds, move on), and its kind for the record. The intro plan becomes `intro` (session 1) and `intro_2` (session 2), both fixed YAML, no LLM.

### B. The needs profile
- **Written by the memory agent** with one tool, `record_needs(area, level, evidence, note)`:
  - `level`: `ok` | `some_difficulty` | `clear_difficulty` | `unclear` (not enough heard; check again later).
  - `evidence`: 1-3 short quotes from the transcript. No quote -> `unclear`. Never a guess.
- **Kept per account** (Firestore, next to the memory), with the date and the session each level came from. A later session can update an area (the agent sees the current profile).
- **The family's word wins:** an area the caregiver page set is not overwritten by the agent (the agent may add a note: "today he sounded different").

### B2. The inferred profile (moved here from 15)
- **The same profile as an uploaded one** (15: `patients/{pid}/caregiver/profile`), with `source: inferred`. The tutor, the memory agent and the plan builder read it the same way (`account_profile`).
- **Written by the memory agent** after each of his sessions, with one tool, `update_profile`, offered **only when there is no uploaded profile**:
  - **About him:** a few short lines (age range if he said, what he does or did, family, where he lives, interests, how he speaks). Only what he said; never guessed. Capped (~8 lines).
  - **His needs:** one line per area from the needs profile (B), e.g. "Naming: some difficulty with proper names".
  - **His vocabulary:** a `Vocabulary:` line with his own names and places (people, towns, places, brands), capped (~40), newest first. His own names (12) are added by code. It feeds the transcription vocabulary like an uploaded profile's.
- Rendered into the profile text by code (not free-written by the model), so it stays short and in the same shape every time.
- **An uploaded profile is never touched.** If the family edits an inferred one on the caregiver page, it becomes `uploaded` and the app stops updating it (a line says so).
- "Forget everything" deletes it with the account's data; "forget memory" keeps it (the family can clear it on the card).
- A first version of this was built in 15 and taken out when 15 was reworked: the `update_profile` tool (about + vocabulary, checked and capped) and its tests can be brought back from that work.

### C. Using it
- **The plan builder** gets a `# HIS NEEDS` section: per area, the level and the family's note. Its prompt: practice the areas with a difficulty, keep a light touch on the `ok` ones, start easy where it's `clear_difficulty`; an `unclear` area gets a small re-check in a later plan.
- **Areas the app can't practice yet** (repetition, understanding, speech clarity) are shown to the family with "not practiced in the app yet". Each needs its own step classes: a later plan per area, if it turns out to matter.
- **A person with no difficulty** (e.g. a relative trying the app): everything `ok` -> plans for general conversation practice, with names and stories as light games. This is also the first test of the whole flow.

### D. The caregiver page
- **A "what he needs" card:** each area, its level, the evidence (expandable), and who set it (the app / the family). The family can change a level and add a note ("his therapist works on repetition").
- **A clear line:** "This is a starting point for practice, built from conversations. It is not a diagnosis; his speech therapist's assessment comes first."

### E. The patient's account (changed 2026-10-15, at Tomer's request)
- He already has an uploaded profile and many sessions: no needs check is run for him, and his profile is never inferred or touched.
- **But his needs are tracked after every session, for the family's eyes only:** the memory agent records each area his session showed (his words as evidence), and each record is kept per session -- a history that shows his progress. With an uploaded profile, the plan builder does NOT get them (they don't change his sessions).
- **Points for his therapist:** rarely (at most two a session, never repeated), the agent suggests a point the family could raise with his speech therapist -- a difficulty that keeps coming back, a clear change, or something the app can't practice -- in Hebrew, with his words. The family marks each one raised / not relevant.
- The family can still set a level by hand; it wins, and the app's view of that area is kept beside it as a note.

## What was built (2026-10-15)
- `CheckStep` (`kind: check`, an `area`, Hebrew `items`): one class, its rules and the answers it needs per area; only in the fixed plans, never built by the plan builder. Steps now carry their own `min_answers` to the page.
- `prompts/intro_needs_1.yaml` (people and places, naming riddles, work, repetition, a place he loves, his goals) and `intro_needs_2.yaml` (a trip, understanding, his week, explaining how to make coffee, music, "פה-טה-קה"). Never a check right after the greeting or two in a row (a test holds it).
- `first_plan`: an uploaded profile gets the old intro; none gets `intro_needs_1`. After it, the next plan is `intro_needs_2` by code (no model). From the third session, the builder plans as usual.
- The memory agent, only without an uploaded profile: `record_needs` (no quote -> `unclear`; the family's level is kept, the app's view is added as a note) and `update_profile` (about, vocabulary); his own names go into the vocabulary by code. The needs (`caregiver/needs`) and the inferred profile (`caregiver/profile`, `source: inferred`, rendered by code) are saved after the session -- never over an uploaded profile, re-checked at save time.
- The plan builder: `# HIS NEEDS` in its context; its prompt no longer describes one patient's level (that is in his profile) but plans from the profile and the needs.
- Caregiver page: "מה הוא צריך" -- per area the level, who set it, his words, a note; the family can set a level (the inferred profile follows at once). The profile card says when the app keeps it up to date.
- Prompts: memory update 0.9, plan builder 0.20.
- Tests: `test_needs_check.py` (9); three older tests now give the patient account an uploaded profile, as in production. `pytest`: 287 passed.
- Then (Tomer, 15.10): his needs are tracked after every session for every account -- a history per area and `suggest_for_therapist`; with an uploaded profile for the family's eyes only (the builder doesn't get them). The card shows each area's levels over the sessions as dots, and the points list with raised / not relevant. Memory update 0.10. `pytest`: 291 passed.
- Not done: the connected-speech check isn't scored as a sequencing baseline (it is recorded as a need); live-testing with a second account is next.
