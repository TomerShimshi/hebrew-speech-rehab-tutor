# Sub-plan 16.5: Practicing the missing areas, once a difficulty is confirmed

Part of the [master design](00-master-design.md). After [16](16-measuring-and-summarizing.md), before [17](17-names-goodbyes-and-excluded-sessions.md). Requested by Tomer (2026-10-15); split from the first plan 16 (2026-10-16). Builds on 16's probe framework. **Reviewed; to build after 16.**

## Why
- **15.5 finds needs in seven areas, but the app practices only three** (conversation, naming, connected speech). Repetition, understanding and speech clarity are shown to the family as "not practiced in the app yet". For an account with a difficulty there, the app sees it and does nothing about it.
- **One hard session isn't a difficulty.** It can be tiredness, a bad day, or a mishearing. Practice should start only once it is confirmed, and gradually.
- For the patient these three areas are preserved (his assessment): this plan is for other accounts, and for him only if the family sets an area on the card (16, A).

## What the research says
- **Repetition:** massed repetition practice improves repetition itself, with modest and variable carry-over to spontaneous speech ([review, Western](https://www.uwo.ca/fhs/lwm/teaching/EBP/2012_13/Spratt_J.pdf); [massed sentence repetition](https://pubs.asha.org/doi/10.1044/2016_AJSLP-15-0151)).
- **Understanding:** the treatment evidence is limited and variable ([Patterson & Purdy](https://www.medbridge.com/educate/courses/auditory-comprehension-deficit-in-aphasia-examining-the-evidence-janet-patterson-mary-purdy)); yes/no questions differ in difficulty in a graded way (a small study: [Carter 1999](https://digital.library.txstate.edu/items/5e191cbd-2958-4e04-89c2-773abc5784e0)), which supports graded practice and graded measuring.
- **Speech clarity:** home and remote programs are feasible and improve communication and participation ratings, but listener-rated clarity changed little in small studies ([Be Clear, telerehabilitation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8870717/)); which cue helps (slower, or louder) differs per person and can be predicted from his baseline ([dysarthria strategies](https://doaj.org/article/6df9c872ad5343ad87470fde1adbf8d2)).
- **Measuring** follows 16: fixed probe sets, a baseline, treated vs untreated, maintenance; and practice gains don't automatically reach conversation (Big CACTUS), so conversation stays measured on its own.

## Goal
1. **A difficulty is confirmed before it is practiced**, and practice is gradual: it grows while the difficulty stays, and fades when it's gone.
2. **Repetition, understanding and clarity can be practiced**, at his level.
3. **Each has a probe set** (16's framework), so the family sees whether practice helps.

## Design

### A. Confirm first, then practice gradually (decided 2026-10-15)
Per area, for an account without an uploaded profile:
1. **Seen once** -> the area is marked "to check again" on the card; nothing is practiced.
2. **A light re-check** in the next 2-3 sessions: one short, natural item of that area inside the talk (a `CheckStep`, 15.5, placed by code).
3. **Confirmed** (a difficulty in 2 of those 3) -> practice starts small: one short step in that area every other session. It grows (every session, longer) while the difficulty stays, and fades out when the probes come back fine for ~3 weeks.
4. **Not confirmed** -> back to "fine", kept on the card as a one-time note.
- The family can confirm or cancel at any stage on the card, and its word wins. (An uploaded profile: practice only when the family sets the level -- 16, A.)
- The status per area (`seen_once`, `rechecking`, `confirmed`, `practicing`, `fading`, `fine`) is kept with the needs (15.5) and shown on the card.

### B. Practice steps for the missing areas (new step classes, builder tools)
Each is a step class (13.5): content by the builder, rules by code, offered to the builder only for an area that is `practicing` (A), at the dose A sets.
- **Repetition** (`add_repetition`): a ladder from his own topics -- words, then short phrases, then a longer sentence (lengths set by code from his last results). She says each once; he repeats; one retry; a warm reaction, never "wrong". Scored by code: the words of his repetition against the target (both transcripts; a low-confidence flag when they disagree).
- **Understanding** (`add_understanding`): graded yes/no questions (simple facts -> about him -> comparisons -> reversible, "האם הכלב רדף אחרי החתול, או החתול אחרי הכלב?"), then two- and three-part spoken requests ("ספר לי X, ואחר כך Y"), then a short story with questions. The memory agent reports right/wrong per item; code scores. Voice only: no "point to" or "show me" tasks.
- **Clarity** (`add_clarity`): phrases from his life said with ONE cue -- slower, or louder, or "clearly, every sound" -- the cue chosen per person from his baseline (which one helped). Measured by how well the transcription caught the target phrase: a rough proxy for a listener (stated as such), plus, optionally, a family rating ("understood all / most / some").

### C. Probe sets for these areas
- Added to 16's `app/probes.py`: a fixed set per area (repetition: words/phrases/sentences; understanding: graded questions; clarity: phrases), with a baseline before practice starts, treated and untreated items, maintenance. Scored by code.

### D. The caregiver page
- Per area: its status (A) with the family's confirm / cancel, the dose, and the probe charts (16's).
