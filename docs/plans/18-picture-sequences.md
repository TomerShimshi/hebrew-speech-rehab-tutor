# Sub-plan 18: Picture sequences, like the therapist's (a draft)

Part of the [master design](00-master-design.md). After [17](17-names-goodbyes-and-excluded-sessions.md). Requested by Tomer (2026-10-13). **A draft: to be planned in detail after the voice-only sequencing of [14](14-sequencing-stories.md) has been tried.**

## Why
His speech therapist works with picture cards: he puts them in order and tells their story chronologically. Plan 14 does the same by voice only. Pictures would bring it closer to her work. For some people pictures help, and for others they don't, so his own results with each will tell ([Doyle 1998](https://aphasiology.pitt.edu/1124/)).

## The idea
- **The tablet shows 4 picture cards out of order.** He arranges them by tapping or dragging, then tells the story.
- **A new step type,** like 14's `SequencingStep`: the picture set, the correct order, and the order on screen. It would be scored the same way (code computes the order score), plus the order he arranged the cards in.

## Open questions (to decide before planning it)
- **Where the pictures come from:** a picture set made once (generated, then reviewed by the family), or a licensed set. They must be clear, adult and unambiguous, with only one right order.
- **How the tutor "sees" them:** their descriptions in her step text, or the images themselves sent to the live model.
- **The screen:** big cards, easy to move for him, and readable on the tablet.
- **Compare with the voice version:** which one gets better practice and results for him.
