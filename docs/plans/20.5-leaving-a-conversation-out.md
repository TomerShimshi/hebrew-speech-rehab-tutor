**Status (30.10): built, not deployed.**
- Built as planned, with these choices:
  - **Exact put-back.** Leaving out keeps exactly what it removed on the session (`left_out_traces`), and putting back re-inserts it, so a recheck's corrections and the needs entries come back too. `restore_session` therefore takes those kept entries, not `probe_results`.

**Goal:** a conversation can be left out of everything that learns, stays visible and marked, and can be put back. Part B: her reaction before a step change is never cut off.

**Architecture:** one filter in `store.list_sessions` (left-out sessions skipped unless asked for), so every reader -- today's and future ones -- is safe by default; exact cleanups (`app/leave_out.py`) for what a processed session left in shared docs (the memory's summaries, the names-game set, the needs); buttons on the caregiver page.

**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, Firestore (and the in-memory store for tests), vanilla JS pages, pytest.

**Spec:** this file (Why, Goal, Design below).

## Global Constraints
- The repo is public: no names, words or transcripts of his in code, tests or this file. Test data is invented.
- No model calls: leaving out and putting back are code only (a put-back that needs the memory agent goes through the existing "process now").
- Nothing is deleted: the transcript, recording, prompt and flags stay.
- Every new caregiver-page text is Hebrew.
- A failure in a cleanup never leaves a half-applied state the page can't show: the session is marked first, the cleanups follow, and each is idempotent (pressing again finishes it).
- Production deploys only on Tomer's explicit "deploy"; commit and push only when he asks.

## Review Focus

2. **Put back after leave out, without a restore:** its names-game results and summary come back once, never twice; nothing is re-run through the memory agent. -> test in Task 2.

## Why
- **No way to leave one conversation out.** A test session on his account, a session that went wrong (noise, a misunderstanding, a technical problem), or someone else speaking can teach the memory, the progress rules and the needs wrong facts. Today the only tools are removing memory items one by one, restoring the whole memory, or a full reset. Tomer wants a button on the session itself.

## Goal
**"Leave this conversation out"** (not a delete): a button next to each conversation on the caregiver page. The conversation stays visible, marked, and can be put back, but nothing learns from it any more.

## Design
- **The button:** on each conversation on the caregiver page, "🚫 לא לכלול בזיכרון ובתכנון", with an inline confirm. A left-out conversation shows a "לא נכלל" chip, and the button becomes "↩ להחזיר".
- **Stored:** `excluded: true`, `excluded_by`, `excluded_at` on the session.
- **One filter instead of a dozen:** `list_sessions(pid, n, *, left_out=False)` skips left-out sessions unless `left_out=True`, and still returns up to `n`. New readers are safe by default.
- **What a left-out conversation no longer feeds:**

  | Where | Reads | When left out |
  |---|---|---|
  | Memory update (incl. the sweep and "process now") | `_claimable`, `pending_*_sessions` | **skipped**, `memory_status: excluded` |
  | The grounding guard (8.5) | `grounding_text` | not counted |
  | His pace (12b), step lengths (13) | `speaking_pace`, `step_minutes` | not counted |
  | Sequencing and summary turns and history (14, 17) | `sequencing_history`, `discourse_turn`, `summarizing_history` | not counted |
  | Progress rules and missing areas (17, 17.5) | `apply_progress`, `missing_areas.practice_turn` | not counted from now on (past decisions in the log stay) |
  | Story probe and his-view timing (17) | `probes.story_due` (via `build_next_plan`'s `list_sessions`) | not counted |
  | "When you last spoke" | `session_timing` | not counted |
  | Recent plans (variety) | `recent_session_plans` | not counted |
  | Rebuild the next lesson | `rebuild_plan` (latest ended session) | built after the latest counted one |
  | Progress graphs and the therapist export | `progress_series` | not shown (a "show left-out" toggle) |
  | The memory's recent summaries | memory doc | its summary removed |
  | The names game's results (12) | check-in set | its results removed |
  | His needs (15.5) | needs doc | its level-history entries and its therapist points removed |
  | The names recheck (20) | `recheck_names_game` | skipped |
  | Voice tuning (09) | runs once, right after /end | unchanged (already applied before anyone can leave it out) |
  | Daily minutes cap (`live_minutes_today`), "already practised today" (`practiced_on`, 11), joining recordings | `list_sessions(..., left_out=True)` | still counts: he did talk |
- **If it was already processed,** its effect on the memory is undone:
  - **It's the latest processed conversation** (`memory.last_session_id == sid`): the page offers "also restore the memory to before this conversation", using the existing restore (version = `sid`, with a backup). This is exact.
  - **Later conversations were processed after it:** restoring would also throw away what they taught. The page says so; its memory items (and an inferred-profile line) can be removed one by one with the existing ✕.
  - The table's exact cleanups (summary, names-game results, needs entries) happen either way.
- **Then the next lesson is rebuilt** without it (the existing rebuild), if the current plan was `built_after_session == sid`.
- **Putting it back** clears the flag, then:
  - **never processed, or its memory was restored away** (`memory_restored_away`): `memory_status: pending`, and the page calls the existing "process now";
  - **otherwise:** its names-game results and summary are put back from the session's own fields (no model call).

### Task 2: Leave out and put back (the actions and cleanups)

**Files:**
- Create `app/leave_out.py`.
- Modify `app/check_in_set.py`, `app/caregiver_api.py`.
- Test: `tests/test_leave_out.py`.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces in `app/check_in_set.py`:
  - `remove_session(current: dict, sid: str) -> bool`: drops that session's results, corrects `asked` and `sessions_played`;
  - `restore_session(current, probe_results, sid, day: dt.date) -> bool`: `record_results` for that day, without duplicates.
- Produces in `app/leave_out.py`:
  ```python
  def leave_out(store, pid: str, sid: str, by: str) -> dict:
      """{"was_processed": bool, "is_latest": bool, "rebuild": bool}. Raises LeaveOutBusy if memory_status is processing."""
  def put_back(store, pid: str, sid: str, *, restored_away: bool) -> dict:
      """{"process": bool} -- True when the page should call the existing /process."""
  ```
  - Cleanups in `leave_out`:
    - its `recent_summaries` entry, then `memory_prompt` re-rendered with `render_memory`;
    - its check-in results (`remove_session`);
    - needs `history` entries and `therapist_points` with `session == sid`.

    It also sets `memory_status: excluded` if the session was never processed (keeping the old status in `memory_status_before`).
  - `put_back`: if the session was never processed, or `restored_away`, it sets `memory_status: pending` and returns `process: True`. Otherwise it re-adds the summary from the session's own `summary`/`mood` fields and the check-in results with `restore_session`.
- Endpoints:
  - `POST /{email}/sessions/{sid}/leave-out` returns `leave_out`'s dict; 409 on `LeaveOutBusy`.
  - `POST /{email}/sessions/{sid}/put-back` with body `{"restored_away": bool}` returns `put_back`'s dict.
  - The existing `/memory/restore` (version = `sid`) sets `memory_restored_away: true` on that session when it is left out.

## Part B: she isn't cut off when she moves to the next step

Added 2026-10-30, from the 30.10 evening session's recording.

**What happens.**
- She says a short reaction to his answer and, while still saying it, calls `next_step`.
- The page answers after a fixed `TRANSCRIPT_CATCHUP_MS` (1.2 s). Gemini then starts the next step's speech.
- Whatever audio of the reaction hadn't reached the page yet is dropped, mid-word. Her transcript still has the whole sentence, because the text runs ahead of the audio.

**The evidence (30.10).** Two cuts, both at a step change:
- At 0:44 the reply went with about 6 s of reaction left.
- At 5:09 it went at 2.4 s of a ~3 s reaction.

Both stopped exactly when her next line began. Neither line was marked interrupted, and the page's only flush is on `interrupted`. At 6:44 the reaction was short and finished before the new step began, and wasn't cut.

**Design.** Answer `next_step` only once her reaction has finished playing:
- The page waits at least `TRANSCRIPT_CATCHUP_MS` (the question guard still needs her transcript).
- It then waits until her audio has stopped (`!s.tutorAudio`) for a short grace (300 ms).
- A cap of 10 s keeps a step from ever waiting forever. This is the same "done" test the hang-up uses (`hangUpAfterGoodbye`).
- `end_session` is unchanged: it already waits for her to finish.

**Files.**
- `static/step_timing.js` (new, dependency-free like `sounds_like.js`): `readyToAnswerStep({ sinceCallMs, herAudioPlaying, quietMs }) -> bool`.
- `static/app.js`: `handleToolCall` polls it (every 100 ms) for a `next_step` instead of the fixed `setTimeout`.
- Test: `tests/js/step_timing.test.mjs`.
