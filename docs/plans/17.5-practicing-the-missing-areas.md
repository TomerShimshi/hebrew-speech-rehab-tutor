# Sub-plan 17.5: Practicing the missing areas, once a difficulty is confirmed

Part of the [master design](00-master-design.md). After [17](17-measuring-and-summarizing.md), before [18](18-one-question-greeting.md). Requested by Tomer (2026-10-15); split from the first plan 16 (2026-10-16; renumbered 16.5 -> 17.5 on 2026-10-19). **Reviewed again after 17 was built (2026-10-22): it reuses 17's progress rules, carry-over and caregiver cards instead of its own. Built 2026-10-22.**

## Why
- **15.5 finds needs in seven areas, but the app practices only three** (conversation, naming, connected speech). Repetition, understanding and speech clarity are shown to the family as "not practiced in the app yet". For an account with a difficulty there, the app sees it and does nothing about it.
- **One hard session isn't a difficulty.** It can be tiredness, a bad day, or a mishearing. Practice should start only once it is confirmed, and gradually.
- For the patient these three areas are preserved (his assessment): this plan is mainly for other accounts. For him, an area is added only if a difficulty is confirmed in his ordinary talk -- gradually, with a notice to the family and their veto (17, A and E).

## What the research says
- **Repetition:** massed repetition practice improves repetition itself, with modest and variable carry-over to spontaneous speech ([review, Western](https://www.uwo.ca/fhs/lwm/teaching/EBP/2012_13/Spratt_J.pdf); [massed sentence repetition](https://pubs.asha.org/doi/10.1044/2016_AJSLP-15-0151)).
- **Understanding:** the treatment evidence is limited and variable ([Patterson & Purdy](https://www.medbridge.com/educate/courses/auditory-comprehension-deficit-in-aphasia-examining-the-evidence-janet-patterson-mary-purdy)); yes/no questions differ in difficulty in a graded way (a small study: [Carter 1999](https://digital.library.txstate.edu/items/5e191cbd-2958-4e04-89c2-773abc5784e0)), which supports graded practice and graded measuring.
- **Speech clarity:** home and remote programs are feasible and improve communication and participation ratings, but listener-rated clarity changed little in small studies ([Be Clear, telerehabilitation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8870717/)); which cue helps (slower, or louder) differs per person and can be predicted from his baseline ([dysarthria strategies](https://doaj.org/article/6df9c872ad5343ad87470fde1adbf8d2)).
- **Measuring** follows 17: fixed items asked the same way, a baseline, practiced items kept apart from measured ones; and practice gains don't automatically reach conversation (Big CACTUS), so conversation stays measured on its own.

## Goal
1. **A difficulty is confirmed before it is practiced**; once confirmed, the area joins 17's progress rules, starting small.
2. **Repetition, understanding and clarity can be practiced**, at his level.
3. **Each is measured on fixed items**, so the family sees whether practice helps.
4. **Sessions don't fill up with exercises.**

## Design

### A. Confirm first (the only new state machine)
Per area (repetition, understanding, clarity), from the needs the memory agent records after every session (15.5):
1. **Seen once** (a `some_difficulty` / `clear_difficulty` level) -> "to check again", shown on the "מה הוא צריך" card; nothing is practiced.
2. **A light re-check** in the next 2-3 sessions: one short, natural item of that area inside the talk -- a `CheckStep` (15.5) queued as a pending check and placed by code, one per plan, the way 17 carries over missed checks (`pending_checks`, `add_one_check`).
3. **Confirmed** (a difficulty in 2 of the 3 sessions that showed the area) -> handed to 17's `app/progress.py` as a new area (B). **Not confirmed** -> back to "fine", kept on the card as a one-time note.
- The status (`seen_once`, `rechecking`, `confirmed`, `not_confirmed`) is kept with the needs, next to the area's level.
- The family can confirm or cancel at any stage on the card; its word wins.
- **For the patient** (an uploaded profile): no re-check items are placed (17, A: no new checks in his sessions) -- confirmation comes from his ordinary talk only (2 of 3 sessions); then the same hand-over, with a notice.

### B. After confirmation: 17's progress rules, nothing new
- The area joins `progress.AREAS` with its own series (C), starting at **level 1, "less" time** -- the gradual start.
- From there it's 17's rules, unchanged: mastered -> harder, less time (and eventually out of the plans); worse -> easier, more time, a point for the therapist; stalled -> another approach. Every change logged; the family's lock and undo (17, E) work as for the other areas.
- **The dose:** "less" = a step in that area every other session; "normal" = every session; "more" = every session, longer. The plan builder's tools check it (like the naming time in 17).

### C. Measuring on fixed item banks (like the story bank)
- **A bank per area**, written once under strict rules, checked by code and a second model call, and read before it goes in (as the 30 stories were, 17), in `prompts/`:
  - **repetition:** words, short phrases and longer sentences, graded by length;
  - **understanding:** graded yes/no questions (simple facts -> comparisons -> reversible) and two- and three-part spoken requests;
  - **clarity:** short everyday phrases.
- **A probe step per area** (like `StoryProbeStep`), placed by code, asking a few bank items the same way every time -- **every ~3rd session** while the area is practiced, plus a baseline in the session it's confirmed.
- **Practice items are never bank items:** the builder writes practice fresh, so what's measured was never practiced -- the measurement stays fair (17's lesson).
- **Scored by code from what the memory agent reports:**
  - **repetition:** what he said for each item -> the words matched against the target;
  - **understanding:** right / wrong per item;
  - **clarity:** how well the transcription caught each phrase -- a rough proxy for a listener, labeled as such, marked low-confidence when the two transcripts disagree (and never counted then, as in 17).

### D. Practice steps (new step classes, builder tools)
Offered to the builder only for an area that progress (B) holds, at its level and dose:
- **Repetition** (`add_repetition`): a ladder from his own topics -- words, short phrases, a longer sentence (lengths from his level). She says each once; he repeats; one retry; a warm reaction, never "wrong".
- **Understanding** (`add_understanding`): graded yes/no questions about his world, then spoken requests in two and three parts, then a short story with questions -- by his level. Voice only: no "point to" or "show me".
- **Clarity** (`add_clarity`): phrases from his life said with ONE cue -- slower, or louder, or "clearly, every sound" -- the cue chosen from his baseline (which one helped).

### E. Not overloading a session
- **At most one step from these areas per session**, taking turns between the practiced areas (the code picks whose turn it is, like the discourse task).
- **Their probes every ~3rd session**, never in the same session as the story probe.
- The builder's time budget includes the practice step; probe steps are outside it (as in 17) -- so a session with a probe is a little longer, to watch.

### F. The caregiver page (17's cards, extended)
- "מה הוא צריך": each area's confirmation status, with confirm / cancel.
- "התאמות שהאפליקציה עשתה": the new areas' level, time, decisions, lock and undo -- as for the others.
- Charts: each area's probe scores over time.

### G. Checked with the evals before reaching anyone
- Run the new steps through `evals/` (text mode): she reacts before moving on, one question at a time, no instructions read aloud, and the step ends only after his answers -- then on a real test account before any patient.

## Later (noted 2026-10-16)

## What was built (2026-10-22)
- **The banks** (`prompts/probe_repetition.yaml` 31, `probe_understanding.yaml` 33, `probe_clarity.yaml` 26): generated by `scripts/make_probe_banks.py` under strict rules, checked by code and a second model call (~15 items dropped as unnatural Hebrew), and read. A measurement takes 4 items with the SAME mix of levels every time (understanding: its top 4 levels), rotating within each level -- a first version shifted the levels between measurements and was fixed before use.
- `app/missing_areas.py`: the confirmation status (windows of 3 sessions; the family's word wins), the re-check step from the bank (via `pending_checks`; none for an uploaded profile), the hand-over to `progress.py` (level 1, less time, logged, a notice to the family), the practice turn (one area a session, by its dose), the probe timing (the baseline, then every ~3rd session, never with the story probe), and scoring by code (words matched; right / wrong).
- `app/progress.py`: the three areas, only once "active"; their levels' meaning for the builder.
- Steps: `RepetitionStep`, `UnderstandingStep`, `ClarityStep` (builder tools, only the area whose turn it is; `finish` requires it) and `AreaProbeStep` (placed by code); `record_area_probe` for the memory agent (+ the extra round when it's missed). The clarity check's rule no longer assumes "פה-טה-קה".
- Caregiver page: the confirmation status with confirm / cancel on "מה הוא צריך"; the areas on "התאמות"; charts. `docs/tools.md` lists the three practice tools.
- **The evals (G):** a neutral plan with the new steps (`evals/plans/missing_areas.json`), 3 runs on Gemini Live: she reacted first at 24/24 step changes, nothing read aloud; the flagged double questions were the understanding items' intended multi-part requests (the judge's prompt now counts one ordered request as one) and the greeting (plan 18). The judge is now batched and cached, to mind the credit.
- Tests: `test_missing_areas.py` (9). `pytest`: 324 passed.
